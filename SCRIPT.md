## THE STORY OF ME

**1 — Title** · *Find your tribe.* Let it sit, then begin.

**2 — My father** · "My father spent his last decades in a single room. The TV, a drink, my mother — and, one by one, no one else."

**3 — The fear** · *slow, quiet.* "When he died I grieved him. But I was also afraid — because I could see myself becoming him." *[pause]*

**4 — Diagnosed at 42** · "Then, at 42, I was diagnosed autistic. Suddenly it all made sense — the edge of every room, twenty years working from home, a family I loved and not one friend of my own."

**5 — The excuse** · "So I did something strange. I set out to design a board game, and to play it I needed people. I invited over some guys who barely knew each other — and honestly, not to help them. Because *I* was lonely, and it was the perfect excuse."

**6 — "Something real"** · *the turn.* "By the end of that first night, men who'd just met were talking about things men never say out loud. One of them told me, 'We talked about something real.' The game gave us permission — and I realized the excuse was the answer. When my father was young he loved pinochle, a game you can't play alone. Somewhere he lost that. I'd just found my way back to it."
> Hinge: "If I needed this that badly — I'm not the only one."

## THE STORY OF US

**7 — The scale** · "About half of us feel lonely. Its health toll rivals smoking. And men are the least likely to admit it. It's the thing half the room is quietly carrying."

**8 — Our neighbors** · "It's not who you'd picture. It's the family that just moved in, the widow down the street, the remote worker, the new parent, the teenager without a tribe. It's you and me."

**9 — Already exists** · "Here's the hope: it's already happening. [X] people have come through our game nights. There are a dozen groups across the area — and every one meets in a borrowed room."

**10 — It just needs a home** · "The community already exists. It just needs a home." *[let it land — this is the pivot]*

## THE STORY OF NOW

**11 — A park you can walk into** · "So that's what we're building. A park you can walk into, rain or shine. Free to enter, open to everyone. Let me show you what's inside."

**12 — The Commons** · "At its heart, an open green you can just walk onto — pull up a chair at a game already going, or simply be around people. The town square we stopped building."

**13 — The Market** · "A ring of local makers — coffee, bread, handmade goods — who don't just sell, but teach and mentor. The living economy that keeps the room warm."

**14 — The Stage** · "A stage for community theatre, movie nights, a kid's first performance — where the room gathers around one shared moment, and someone finally gets to be seen."

**15 — The Game Library** · "And the thing that started it all: hundreds of games, always free to play, someone always ready to teach you. The lowest-pressure way in the world to sit down with a stranger and get up a friend."

**16 — For the disconnected** · *slow.* "Because in the end, this is a place for the people every other place leaves out — the ones who feel disconnected from their own community. The person at the edge of the room. The one I used to be." *[let it land]*

**17 — The ask** · "The community is here. The need is real. What's missing is the home — and a partner to help build it. I can't give my father back those years, but I can help build the place I wish he'd had. Help us open the doors. You can give right here." *[gesture to QR]* "Thank you."
