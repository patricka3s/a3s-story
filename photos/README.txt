PHOTOS — drop your real A3S photos in THIS folder
==================================================

The presentation looks for the files below. Until a file is here, the slide
shows a labeled placeholder. As soon as you add a file with the matching name,
it appears automatically — just refresh the page.

Use landscape (wide) photos where possible. JPG or PNG both work; if you use
PNG, keep the .jpg name OR update the filename in index.html.

FILES THE SLIDES LOOK FOR
-------------------------
story.jpg        Slide 2 (Your story) — you at a game night, or a candid that
                 connects to your personal story. A real, human moment.

vision.jpg       Slide 5 (The vision) — the warmest community photo you have:
                 people leaning over a board, laughing, mid-game.

gamenight-1.jpg  Slide 7 (Proof) — three shots of your actual game nights /
gamenight-2.jpg  community. Variety is good: a wide room shot, a close-up of
gamenight-3.jpg  hands/faces, a group.

TIPS
----
- Faces and genuine expressions matter more than image quality. A slightly
  grainy photo of real people connecting beats a polished stock image.
- Get permission before showing recognizable faces, especially any minors.
- To use different filenames, open index.html and edit the src="photos/..."
  paths (there's one per photo).
